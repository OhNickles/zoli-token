AUTHORITY POLICY

The current Devnet mint authority remains active because ZOLI is still being developed and tested. The site will not describe the supply as permanently fixed while that authority exists.

The freeze authority is already revoked. That means there is no mint freeze authority capable of freezing ZOLI token accounts.

Any decision to revoke the remaining mint authority will be tested first and documented before it is made. Authority revocation can be irreversible.

GOOD CATS HELP OTHER CATS
