THE ORIGINAL GOOD CAT

THIS IS ZOLI.

The cat behind the coin, and the reason the project exists at all.

THE CAT

Ginger tabby. White paws. Apartment supervisor. Professional observer of humans.

ZOLI began as a small internet monument to her: something playful, personal and deliberately unserious.

THE IDEA

A token can be more than a ticker. ZOLI is being designed as a small social object — something cat people can give, collect and use to recognise excellent cats.

No complicated tokenomics. No invented utility for the sake of it. Start with a good cat and build from there.

THE PURPOSE

WHAT IS ZOLI FOR?
