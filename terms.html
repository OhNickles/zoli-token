WHAT ZOLI IS

ZOLI is currently an experimental Solana Devnet community token created in honour of a cat named Zoli. It is being developed for entertainment, community participation and cat-appreciation use cases.

NO GUARANTEED VALUE

No promise is made that ZOLI will have monetary value, become tradeable, increase in price, produce income or deliver a financial return. Do not treat statements on this site as financial advice.

DEVELOPMENT CAN CHANGE

Features, token mechanics, website content, authorities and planned use cases may change while the project remains in development. Material changes should be reflected on the Transparency page.

CHARITABLE SUPPORT

No public charitable fundraising is currently active. Any future donation campaign will identify the intended beneficiary and the applicable donation formula before funds are solicited.

WALLET RESPONSIBILITY

Users are responsible for protecting their own wallet credentials and reviewing transactions before signing them. ZOLI will never request a seed phrase or private key.
